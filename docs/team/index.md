# Orbit Research Group

## About

We study reacting flows, porous media and the numerical methods that hold them
together. This page is maintained by the group's communications lead.

## Selected Work

**Turbulent Boundary Layers Over Porous Additively Manufactured Surfaces**<br>
Ana Reyes · Tomas Vild† · Priya Nair† · <mark>Ivo Marchetti</mark><br>
*Journal of Fluid Mechanics* 2024 · [paper](https://doi.org/10.5555/jfm.2024.0042) · [code](https://github.example.com/orbit/abl-suite)<br>
- Quantifies roughness transitions across eight printed pore geometries.
- Reports a wall-law correction that holds to 3% up to Re=1.8e5.
- Releases the measurement suite and post-processing scripts.

**A Variational Framework for Coupled Transport in Porous Media**<br>
<mark>Ivo Marchetti</mark> · Hana Sato · Bo Lindqvist† · Mina Farouk†<br>
*Transport in Porous Media* 2023 · [paper](https://doi.org/10.5555/tpm.2023.118) · [dataset](https://doi.org/10.5555/zenodo.2023.7714)<br>
- Unifies advection, diffusion and reaction in a single saddle-point form.
- Proves well-posedness for heterogeneous conductivity fields.
- Compares against three established upscaling schemes.

**Learned Closures for Low-Dimensional Manifold Combustion Models**<br>
Sofia Brann · <mark>Ivo Marchetti</mark> · Tobias Renner† · Amara Diallo†<br>
*Proceedings of the Combustion Institute* 2022 · [paper](https://doi.org/10.5555/pci.2022.091) · [slides](https://github.example.com/orbit/closure-talk)<br>
- Trains a manifold closure that stays stable outside the fitting envelope.
- Cuts stiff-solver iterations by 41% on the counterflow cases.
- Publishes the training pipeline and the reduced mechanism.

**Stochastic Galerkin Reduced Models for Reactive Mixing**<br>
Mira Okonkwo† · Bo Lindqvist · <mark>Ivo Marchetti</mark><br>
*SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing* 2021 · [paper](https://doi.org/10.5555/sisc.2021.330) · [code](https://github.example.com/orbit/sgrm)<br>
- Builds a reduced basis adapted to random inflow conditions.
- Bounds the variance error independently of the Peclet number.
- Provides an offline-online split for repeated parameter sweeps.
